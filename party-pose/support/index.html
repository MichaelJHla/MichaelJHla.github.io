Party Pose

Support

Need help with Party Pose? Check the answers below, or email jhlastala@outlook.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I play?

One person, the director, holds the phone sideways and sees a stick figure over the camera picture. The other, the poser, stands in front of the rear camera. The director coaches the poser into the pose. The poser's screen shows red (not close), yellow (getting close) or green (matched). Hold green to lock the pose in. Each game is 5 poses.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Party Pose is designed for iPhone Duo: open it, the director uses the inside screen and the poser watches the outer screen. It also works on other iPhones running iOS 27.1 or later. The poser can then watch a TV or monitor connected with AirPlay or a cable, or the director calls out the colours.

The camera isn't working

  • Make sure camera access is on: Settings → Party Pose → Camera.
  • Stand back far enough that the poser's whole body, head to feet, is in view.
  • Good lighting helps the app see body positions.
  • Try the 0.5× button on screen to fit more of the room.

The outer screen on iPhone Duo is blank

Open the phone fully and make sure the game is running with the camera on. If it still stays blank, open Settings → Display diagnostics in the app and email us what it shows.
