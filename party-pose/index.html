Party Pose

A two-player posing party game for iPhone Duo.

You direct, they pose. A stick figure appears over the camera picture on your screen, and only you can see it. Coach your friend into the pose while their screen glows red, yellow or green to show how close they are. Hold green to score, and match all 5 poses before the clock runs out.

Features

  • Built for iPhone Duo: you use the inside screen, the poser watches the outer screen.
  • Easy, Medium and Hard games, plus a Practice mode where you pick the time and the kinds of pose.
  • Live body tracking that runs entirely on your phone. Video never leaves the device.
  • Game Center leaderboards for Easy, Medium and Hard, so you can see how your friends did.
