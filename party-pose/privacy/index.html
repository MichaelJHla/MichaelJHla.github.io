Party Pose

Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Party Pose is an iPhone game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and who it is shared with. In short: the camera picture never leaves your phone, the developer collects no personal information, and the app shows ads from Google AdMob unless you buy the ad-free version.

Camera

Party Pose uses your iPhone's rear camera to see the person posing and check how closely they match the on-screen stick figure. The video is processed only on your device, frame by frame, to find body joint positions (using Apple's on-device Vision framework).

  • The video is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere, including to the developer.
  • No photos or videos are stored, and no images of people are collected.
  • You can turn camera access off at any time in the iPhone Settings app. The game can't be played without it.

Information the developer collects

None. Party Pose has no accounts of its own (the optional Game Center sign-in is Apple's, described below), and no analytics or tracking of its own. The developer doesn't run any servers that receive data from the app, and doesn't sell or share personal information.

Your game settings (such as difficulty and practice options) and whether you've bought the ad-free version are stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Party Pose shows a full-screen ad after each finished game. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect information such as:

  • your device's IP address, which can indicate an approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data related to ads;
  • identifiers used for advertising, where your device settings allow it.

Party Pose does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency), so the iPhone's advertising identifier isn't shared for that purpose.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices in the app. Depending on where you live, a similar privacy-choices option may be shown for other regions where the law requires it.

Google's handling of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Ad-free purchase

You can remove ads with a one-time in-app purchase. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details or Apple Account information, only Apple's confirmation that the purchase was made, which is checked on your device. Once you've bought the ad-free version, the app no longer loads Google's advertising software.

Apple's handling of purchase information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Game Center leaderboards (optional)

Party Pose uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards. If you're signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the score from each finished game (not practice rounds) is sent to Game Center with your Game Center player profile. It then appears on the Party Pose leaderboards for you, your Game Center friends and other players, according to your Game Center privacy settings.

  • Game Center is run by Apple. The developer doesn't receive or store your Apple Account details, contacts or friends list. Like other players, the developer can see the leaderboards, which show Game Center nicknames and scores.
  • If you aren't signed in to Game Center, the game works the same, without leaderboards.
  • You can sign out, or choose who can see your Game Center profile, in the iPhone Settings app under Game Center.

Apple's handling of Game Center information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

Party Pose is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please get in touch using the email below.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off camera access in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Change your ad consent in the app under Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices (where available).
  • Limit ad personalization through your device settings and Google's My Ad Center.
  • Remove ads entirely with the ad-free purchase.
  • Sign out of Game Center, or change who can see your scores, in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Delete the app to remove all settings it stored on your device.

Because the developer doesn't collect personal information, there is no developer-held data to access or delete. For data held by Google for advertising, use Google's tools linked above.
