Party Pose

Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Party Pose is an iPhone game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and who it is shared with. In short: the camera picture never leaves your phone, and the app collects no personal information. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no third-party software in the app. Optional leaderboards use Apple's Game Center.

Camera

Party Pose uses your iPhone's rear camera to see the person posing and check how closely they match the on-screen stick figure. The video is processed only on your device, frame by frame, to find body joint positions (using Apple's on-device Vision framework).

  • The video is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere, including to the developer.
  • No photos or videos are stored, and no images of people are collected.
  • You can turn camera access off at any time in the iPhone Settings app. The game can't be played without it.

Information the developer collects

None. Party Pose has no accounts of its own (the optional Game Center sign-in is Apple's, described below), and no analytics or tracking. The developer doesn't run any servers that receive data from the app, and doesn't sell or share personal information.

Your game settings (such as difficulty and practice options) are stored only on your device.

Game Center leaderboards (optional)

Party Pose uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards. If you're signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the score from each finished game (not practice rounds) is sent to Game Center with your Game Center player profile. It then appears on the Party Pose leaderboards for you, your Game Center friends and other players, according to your Game Center privacy settings.

  • Game Center is run by Apple. The developer doesn't receive or store your Apple Account details, contacts or friends list. Like other players, the developer can see the leaderboards, which show Game Center nicknames and scores.
  • If you aren't signed in to Game Center, the game works the same, without leaderboards.
  • You can sign out, or choose who can see your Game Center profile, in the iPhone Settings app under Game Center.

Apple's handling of Game Center information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

Party Pose is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please get in touch using the email below.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off camera access in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Sign out of Game Center, or change who can see your scores, in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Delete the app to remove all settings it stored on your device.

Because the developer doesn't collect personal information, there is no developer-held data to access or delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
